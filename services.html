ABOUT OUR CENTRE

Bhopal Neuropsychiatric Clinic & Diagnostic Center

Bhopal Neuro Psychiatric Centre is a specialized mental health and neuro-diagnostic centre committed to delivering high-quality psychiatric care through a combination of clinical expertise, advanced technology, and compassionate treatment.

Our centre provides comprehensive services for a wide range of mental health, behavioral, emotional, developmental, and neurocognitive disorders. We focus on accurate diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and long-term recovery for every patient and their family.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, ethical, evidence-based psychiatric and psychological care while promoting mental wellness and improving quality of life for individuals and families.

Our Vision

To be a leading mental health centre recognized for excellence in patient care, innovative neuromodulation research, academic training, and ethical clinical practice.
